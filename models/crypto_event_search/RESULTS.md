## Interpretation and limitations

Only BTC daily random-wallet placebo and ETH daily late-whale flow advanced.
The placebo's ROI reversed sign. ETH late-whale retained a positive after-cost
mean (+0.170845), but raw p=0.025650 and Holm p=0.051299 fail the registered
round-four 0.01 threshold. No smart-wallet or spot-model hypothesis advanced;
the pooled smart-wallet development mean was -0.097940, p=0.994263. The other
41 registered hypotheses' holdout returns remain uncomputed.

- Primary metric is equal-weight date-cluster after-cost ROI, including cash zeros, not annualized return. Contracts are averaged within event, events within family/date, then families within date. Event, contract and executed-position counts are in results.csv.
- Development requires positive ROI and one-sided p<0.10. Holdout requires the same positive sign and Holm-adjusted one-sided p<0.01 across every advanced family/strategy and pooled hypothesis. This is **round 4 of a search sequence**; stricter alpha does not erase cumulative search risk.
- Dashes mean not evaluated, never zero. Non-advanced holdout strategy performance remains uncomputed. The sole pooled test is smart-wallet flow. Placebo passes are negative-control warnings, not evidence of skill.
- The sample is at most 120 evenly spread events per identified recurring production family; see FEASIBILITY.md and the locked coverage CSVs. Weekly families fail the 60-event threshold. Chainlink TWAP rules and an hourly object in a weekly series are excluded from the aligned Binance sample.
- Three-year retention, recurring-series discovery, survivorship, conservative Gamma settlement-time proxies and a single seeded placebo limit generality. Date clustering does not remove dependence between nearby dates.
- Per-family split boundaries can differ, so development and holdout dates may overlap across families. No cross-crypto-family skill/calibration is shared, but phase-specific clustering does not eliminate cross-phase correlation.
- First participant prices are not executable order-book quotes. $0.02/share is the registered flat cost proxy, not historical fee/spread/slippage/fill reconstruction, including fee-enabled markets. A statistical pass is not proof of executable profit.
- The spot model assumes zero drift/rates and lognormal terminal spot with trailing realized volatility. It omits volatility risk premia, jumps, intraday seasonality and changing regimes. Only completed pre-cutoff candles and already-known strike prices enter; no resolution candle close is used as a predictor.
- Calibration is frozen on prior-resolved development events; wallet skill updates only from prior-resolved FOMC/macro and same-family crypto trades. The earlier FOMC/macro studies are reused training sources, not fresh validation.
